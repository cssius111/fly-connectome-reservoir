# M1.5 environment and interaction-data foundation

M1.6 now defaults to ROOM. This document describes the preserved M1.5 GAME
preset; add `--arena game` to its non-LAB launch examples. See [ROOM.md](ROOM.md).

M1.4 was committed separately as `fe2efb758a10802442edabad01e5a976f4c58b16`.
M1.5 changes remain uncommitted pending human playtesting. No optimizer,
LearnedEscapePolicy implementation, RL update or plasticity is present.

## Launch and mode boundaries

```powershell
Set-Location D:\Projects\flybrain-lab
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m game.app
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m game.app --windowed --seed 101
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m game.app --mode lab --windowed
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m game.app --mode evaluation --seed 101 --windowed
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m game.app --mode training --seed 101 --windowed
```

Execution mode and physical arena are separate. `--arena lab|game` overrides
the default arena; `--config PATH` selects an exact custom physical config.
Use `--arena lab` alongside a custom LAB config for its windowed default.
`--fullscreen`/`--windowed` override presentation. F11 remains available.

| Mode | Default arena | Default seed | Recording | Updates now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLAY | GAME | new random base seed per launch | on | disabled |
| EVALUATION | GAME | config seed | on | disabled |
| TRAINING | GAME | new random base seed per launch | on | disabled; data collection only |
| LAB | LAB | config seed | off | disabled |

`--seed N` makes every mode reproducible for identical tick-level inputs.
R/Enter advances to `base_seed + 1000 * restart_count`, clears pause and resets
all neural/internal histories. EVALUATION is frozen; TRAINING records a future
permission boundary, not an operational learner. `--no-record` disables logs;
`--record` enables them. Headless `--smoke N` disables default recording and
fullscreen, retaining the selected arena. PLAY hides the HUD initially; H
reveals it. No record contains a trained checkpoint yet.

## GAME scale versus LAB

`game_config.json` is the unchanged M1.4 LAB preset. `game_play_config.json`
is the M1.5 GAME preset; neither mutates the frozen reservoir `config.json`.

| Quantity | LAB | GAME |
|---|---:|---:|
| Logical arena | 1280 × 720 | 3840 × 2160 |
| Body length | 24 units | 24 units |
| Arena in body lengths | 53.33 × 30 | 160 × 90 |
| Cruise | 160 units/s; 6.67 BL/s | 216 units/s; 9 BL/s |
| Speed safety cap | 1000 units/s | 1000 units/s; 41.67 BL/s |
| Swatter diameter | 112 units; 4.67 BL | 288 units; 12 BL |
| Default presentation | windowed | fullscreen |

GAME has nine times the area, while its body occupies 0.625% of arena width.
The simulation never adopts monitor pixels as physical dimensions. A logical
1280-wide rendering canvas is letterboxed to the display, preserving aspect
ratio and world-space pointer mapping. A 1920×1080 display draws a 12 px body
and 144 px swatter diameter; 2560×1440 draws 16 px and 192 px. At 1280×720 these
are 8 px and 96 px. The HUD uses its own legible canvas scale. No millimeter,
aerodynamic or real-flight speed calibration is claimed.

## Seeded spawning

PLAY/GAME samples a body position uniformly inside a rectangle inset by the
wall margin + collision radius + six body lengths: x in [203,3637], y in
[203,1957]. Rejection sampling excludes a 227-unit radius around the initial
swatter center. No quadrant is selected specially. Initial phase is IDLE;
there is no pre-existing strike or hidden impending-click state.

Body heading is uniform over [-pi,pi), initial velocity direction differs by
at most 12°, and initial speed is 0.8–1.05 times cruise. Curvature phases and
remaining spontaneous-turn wait are also seeded. No active pulse is imposed
at spawn. Independent RNG streams separate spawn/curvature, flight events,
world drift and neuronal noise. The same seed and input sequence reproduce
state, including evaluation runs. LAB retains its fixed pose. Calibration
reset explicitly selects the fixed pose, zero velocity and frozen flight clocks.

## Directional swatter and perception

WORLD samples pointer positions once per simulation tick and fits velocity by
least squares over approximately 180 ms. At click, the recent direction and
speed are latched. Speed is capped at the ordinary tracking speed (1800 units/s);
below 20 units/s it becomes a stationary strike. Sweep speed is 45% of that
speed, capped at 1000 units/s. During wind-up/active phases:

`position += sweep_velocity * sin(pi * strike_elapsed / strike_duration) * dt`

Later pointer changes cannot retarget a committed stroke. Wind-up lasts 0.18 s
and active contact 0.08 s; height lowers from 320 to 150 to 24 units. Existing
phase recovery and a circular lethal footprint remain game conventions. The
paddle sprite/handle rotates along its physical approach axis. An orientation-
dependent projected span modifies the scalar retinal geometry: up to 25%
foreshortening while tilted, vanishing at face-on contact. This is a simplified
geometric projection, not a reconstructed 3D retina or a pixel-based eye.

The fly still receives only Retina(theta, theta_dot, azimuth). It receives no
mouse velocity, attack-angle label, click-imminent signal, phase flag or world
coordinate. The graph and fixed DNp01/DNa02 policy have not been replaced by
conventional swatter AI. Directional geometry is allowed to change neural
input solely through retinal consequences.

## Curvature, saccades and sideslip

GAME inter-saccadic yaw is a deterministic sum of two low-frequency sinusoids:

`0.16*sin(2*pi*t/7 + phase_1) + 0.06*sin(2*pi*t/13 + phase_2)` rad/s.

Phases come from the episode seed; t advances only while flight is enabled.
The maximum contribution is 0.22 rad/s; adjacent 20 ms ticks change this yaw
by less than 0.005 rad/s in the regression. No independent frame-wise random
steering is added. Major heading changes remain discrete M1.4 half-sine
saccades with the same angle/rate bounds, seeded interval mixture and priority
arbitration. The larger arena reduces the fraction of time spent near walls;
WallCue remains strictly local, with no map or exit waypoint.

Translational velocity already has inertia. GAME additionally relaxes its
angular mismatch toward body heading exponentially with a 0.28 s time constant,
then caps the mismatch at 80°. Speed magnitude is preserved by this angular
step, while ordinary damping still relaxes speed toward cruise. Body heading
is never assigned from instantaneous velocity. The hard cone is a game-engine
safety limit and may create an acceleration discontinuity when reached; it is
not a lift or banking model. There is no arbitrary sideways drift indefinitely,
position teleport, CFD or airflow field. Optional airflow was not needed.

## Interaction records and exact observation schema

Default profile directory: `artifacts/game/player-default/`. Use
`--record-dir PATH` for a separate player/profile. Run directories are unique:
`runs/<UTC timestamp>-<random suffix>/`. Profile counters persist across runs;
use one game process per profile directory (no multi-process counter locking).

- `manifest.json`: schema, full actual config, provenance, policy identity,
  observation schema, timing alignment, sample stride and recording conventions.
- `episodes.jsonl`: episode seed, spawn pose/velocity/timing, mode, provenance,
  termination, survival/strike counters and right-censoring.
- `policy_samples.jsonl`: whitelisted observation, selected Action, executed
  actuator state, maneuver type and post-step outcomes. Normally every fifth
  tick (10 Hz); all pulse deltas, escape requests, strike events and wall contacts
  are retained. Outcomes include hit/miss/alive, survival time, wall contact and
  diagnostic pathology flags.
- `world_debug.jsonl`: every active episode tick, pointer/swatter world state,
  click occurrence, fly pose, Retina, neural summaries, injection voltages and
  actual fired-cell counts in the balanced LC4/LPLC2 populations. This file is
  analysis data, never policy input.
- `strikes.jsonl`: seed/spawn/initial heading, latched attack angle/speed,
  pre-strike and strike retinal trajectories, summary statistics, LC4/LPLC2
  fired-cell counts (distinct from injection drive), DNp01/DNa02, steering,
  hit/miss/incomplete status, survival after observed threat onset and right-censoring.
- `profile.json`: episodes played, strikes survived, hits received,
  `policy_checkpoint: null`, `learning_updates: 0`. These are counters, not a
  trained or personalized policy.

The exact future observation is:

```json
{
  "neural": {
    "dnp01_left": 0.0, "dnp01_right": 0.0,
    "dna02_left": 0.0, "dna02_right": 0.0
  },
  "motion": {
    "forward_speed": 0.0, "lateral_speed": 0.0,
    "yaw_rate": 0.0, "saccade_remaining": 0.0
  },
  "behavior_state": "CALM",
  "history": []
}
```

History contains at most four previous 20 ms frames with exactly `neural`,
`motion` and `behavior_state` (no nested history). Thus five frames including
current span 80 ms. The behavior state is sampled **before** the current policy
call; neural activity and MotionState are the current legitimate policy inputs.
The full descending trace remains in MotorState for existing interfaces, but
is not included in this compact version-1 future observation. Retina stays in
analysis logs, not this policy schema. Coordinates, pointer velocity, absolute
heading, seed and future outcomes are excluded from the observation.

`Action` records escape, lateral/forward direction, turn, strength and signed
saccade request. Separate actuator fields report active requested angle,
duration/remaining time, pulse yaw delta and total yaw rate; completion clears
active pulse fields, but preserves that tick's delta. Body physics enforces
limits. Outcomes are **labels after the action**, not policy features.

Recorder input copies are read-only with respect to simulation. A regression
compares recorded and unrecorded neural/physical trajectories exactly. State
history and pre-strike buffers are bounded; JSONL streams go to disk and remain
Git-ignored. Per-strike summaries are buffered until the episode ends. Long
recordings consume disk; `--no-record` remains available. Unclean termination
can lose unflushed rows, the pending episode summary and profile counter update;
completed JSONL rows remain inspectable.

Threat onset is an offline convention: first theta>0.1 and theta_dot>0.2 in the
0.5 s pre-strike/strike window, otherwise click. Survival runs from that onset
to episode end; a living fly at restart/quit is right-censored. This is not a
physiological onset estimate or a causal survival effect of one attack.

## Calibration and future learning

LAB retains its matching calibration at threshold 1.45. GAME uses independent
full/summary files named `calibration_game.json` and has a measured threshold
of 1.35. Recalibrate GAME with:

```powershell
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\calibrate_escape.py --config game_play_config.json --trials 28
```

The fixed-fly protocol remains fixed. It calibrates stationary-offset strikes,
not the entire directional encounter distribution. A full configuration hash
prevents mixing records from different geometries; stale artifacts still cannot
override a matching record. Directional variation is assessed separately by
`python tools/encounter_sanity.py`, not used to optimize the threshold or hit rate.

The future M2 architecture is MaleCNS perception → legitimate neural/internal
state → LearnedEscapePolicy → bounded maneuver parameters → body physics.
Candidate strategic outputs are turn bias, saccade tendency/magnitude, escape
strength, persistence and repertoire selection. The future learner must not
output x/y positions, arbitrary vx/vy, direct mouse responses or teleportation.
**RL may learn strategy; the flight model must enforce body physics.** No such
learner or optimizer is implemented in this milestone.

## Biological grounding and modeling assumptions

1. **Direct biological/connectomic evidence:** MaleCNS connectivity and cell
   annotations. Original data: 25,582,938 connections; game runtime with incoming
   sensory edges removed: 25,088,107. It is not the full untouched connectome.
2. **Biologically motivated phenomenological approximation:** coherent flight,
   modest curvature, discrete saccades, brief heading/velocity separation,
   local boundary exploration and the manually specified neural decoder. None
   of these exact numeric algorithms is asserted to be a known neural circuit.
3. **Pure game-engine convention:** logical body scale, randomized encounters,
   larger arena, circular paddle contact, directional stroke envelope, damping,
   sideslip cone, speed/yaw limits, presentation and hard containment.

The model omits full 3D roll/pitch/banking, lift, wings and retinal image
processing. Headings and rates are game-scale choices. Scripted hits are not
learning, biological validation or evidence of connectome superiority.

## Five-to-ten-minute human acceptance checklist

1. **0–1 min:** launch default fullscreen PLAY. Judge available space, small-body
   visibility and paddle size. Hide/show H; inspect that HUD remains readable.
2. **1–2 min:** use R/Enter repeatedly. Expect new positions, headings and initial
   courses; no wall overlap or unavoidable spawn strike. Note the displayed seed.
3. **2–3 min:** leave the swatter away. Look for modest smooth curvature between
   discrete turns, occasional straight flight, no jitter or prolonged sideways slide.
4. **3–5 min:** approach from left/right/diagonals, then slowly accelerate before
   clicking. The paddle should sweep along recent motion and produce visibly
   varied neural HUD responses. A stationary click should remain usable.
5. **5–7 min:** track directly, then lead the trajectory. Both prediction and
   occasional evasion should be plausible; the larger paddle must not trivialize
   every attack. Scripted pursuit is intentionally not a difficulty benchmark.
6. **7–8 min:** inspect a few wall encounters and rapid escapes. Brief sideslip is
   allowed; sustained wall hugging, oscillation or extreme sliding is a failure.
7. **8–10 min:** toggle F11, pause, R/Enter and quit normally. Confirm a new run
   directory and updated profile counters. Replay a troublesome seed with
   `--mode evaluation --seed N --windowed` and describe the input sequence.

Stop for owner review. Do not commit final M1.5, push it or begin M2 learning.
