# M1.8-B3a validation: the ecology reacts to executed responses

Status: **accepted by the user on 2026-10-21** after the human playtest in section 9.

Baseline: the accepted runtime `feature/m1-8-b2b-iii` @ `c4f9b8f` (ROOM `config_version`
18). Design and acceptance checks were preregistered in
[game/M1_8_B3A_DESIGN.md](../../game/M1_8_B3A_DESIGN.md) (`d654cdb`) before any code
changed. Implementation: `0e463bc`.

## 1. What changed

`Session.tick` now hands the ecology its own ThreatState. Under
`ecology_threat_input.mode = executed_response_v1` the fixed policy's ALERT state alone, with
no saccade requested on the tick and no ALERT saccade executing, reaches the ecology as CALM.
ESCAPE and executed alert responses pass unchanged. The lifecycle keeps the accepted threat.
Without the block (LAB, GAME, older configurations) the accepted mapping applies; an unknown
mode is rejected. The mapping is class C.

Unchanged: the fixed policy (ALERT gate, alert turn, saccades, reported behaviour state), the
N4B1C decoder, the N4B5R geometry, the M1.7.1 swatter, the M1.8-A lifecycle and its threat
input, the B1-B2b-iii kinematics, the policy observation whitelist and recorder schema 4.

Files: `game/session.py`, `game_room_config.json` (`config_version` 18 -> 19, the new block,
the record path), `results/game/calibration_room_m1_8_b3a.json` (written by
`tools/b3a_record.py`), `test_game_b3a.py` (new), `test_game_kinematics.py` and
`test_game_lifecycle.py` (provenance chain), `tools/paired_free_flight_check.py` (new).

## 6. Check 5: paired free flight

`tools/paired_free_flight_check.py` (the B2b-iii method): 80 fresh seeds 9201-9280, 180 s
each, no player, the pointer parked, one Numba thread, under `config_version` 18 (`c4f9b8f`)
and 19 with the same code; every escape classified with the N4B4 counterfactual.

| | Config 18 (B2b-iii) | Config 19 (B3a) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alive minutes | 240.0 | 240.0 |
| Escapes (rate, exact 95 % upper bound) | 6 (0.025/min, 0.049) | 10 (0.042/min, 0.071) |
| A: no LC4/LPLC2 drive in the 120 ms before | 1 | 1 |
| B: never within 310 units in 1.5 s without the escape | 2 (upper 0.026) | 1 (upper **0.020**) |
| C: near pass, mostly apparent-size expansion | 1 | 0 |
| D: near pass, mostly range expansion (self-approach) | 2 | **8** (upper 0.060) |
| Perched escapes | 0 | 0 |
| Escape channel | FAST 6 | FAST 10 |

- **The preregistered criterion holds:** category B under the candidate is 1 in 240 min,
  upper bound 0.020/min, below 0.1/min.
- **Self-approach escapes (D) rise from 2 to 8**, the risk the design note named: with
  TRANSIT at about a quarter of alive time the fly flies fast toward the parked paddle more
  often. All ten D events fall in seeds whose escape ticks differ between the arms.
  Conditioning on the totals, the exact two-sided binomial test gives p = 0.11 for D (2
  against 8) and p = 0.45 for all escapes (6 against 10), so the increase is plausible but
  not statistically established at this sample size. D is tracked, not a failure, under the
  adopted categories.
- The single category A event is the same in both arms (seed 9232, tick 6485, FAST path,
  zero drive, about 2,080 units from the paddle): a spontaneous summed-path event of the
  accepted N4B1C decoder that precedes any B3a divergence, not a B3a effect.
- 68 of the 80 seeds give identical escape ticks in both arms.
- For reference, the B2b-iii check on other fresh seeds (9101-9180) gave 9 escapes under
  config 18 with D 5; combining both sets is not a preregistered comparison and is not used.

## 9. Check 7: human playtest

One session, played by the user on 2026-10-21 (`20261021T054015.211928Z-9e97d496`, seed 255,
recorded from an LF worktree of `4402341`, `config_version` 19, `executed_response_v1`, 4 Numba
threads; the recording's dirty flag comes from `requirements-lock.txt` and
`results/verification.json`, which the game does not read). The recording stays private and
git-ignored. It was summarized read-only with `tools/playtest_flight_summary.py` (`6784092`),
which on a no-player recording of free-flight seed 9278 labels the paired check's category D
escape at tick 6048 as a fly-driven, range-dominated self-approach.

The player was asked to watch whether the fly cruises or transits steadily instead of being
interrupted every second, and whether it rushes into a parked paddle unnaturally often. The
player reported no visible rushing into the paddle, and transit that is fast but not very fast.

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded / alive / airborne time | 219.1 / 213.8 / 180.2 s, 2 episodes |
| Lifecycle share of alive time | airborne 78.5 %, perched 15.7 %, landing approach 5.8 % |
| Airborne ecology share | EXPLORE 36.1 %, TRANSIT 17.9 %, ODOR_TRACK 15.3 %, RECOVER 12.4 %, ODOR_SEARCH 10.0 %, ESCAPE 7.6 %, ALERT 0.7 % |
| Mean bout | EXPLORE 2.33 s (28 bouts), TRANSIT 3.23 s (10 bouts) |
| TRANSIT speed after 1 s in the bout | mean 9.6, p90 12.2, max 12.7 BL/s (EXPLORE mean 6.8 BL/s) |
| TRANSIT net displacement per bout | mean 23.2, max 50.4 BL (arena width 160 BL) |
| Policy alert onsets / ecology ALERT entries | 226 / 32 |
| Ecology ALERT entries without a requested or executing saccade | **0** |
| Escape onsets | 29: 2 during a strike, 19 with the hovering paddle moving toward the fly, 8 dominated by apparent-size change at 27-365 units, **0 fly-driven and range-dominated** |
| Near passes within 310 units, no strike | 15, of which 5 fly-driven; none followed by an escape within 0.5 s |
| Paddle parked while the fly was airborne | 132.5 s; 0.07 self-approach escapes expected at the paired check's rate (P(none) = 0.93) |

- **The B3a property holds in human play.** No ecology ALERT entry arrives without an executed
  alert response; 194 of the 226 policy alert onsets no longer interrupt the ecology.
- **No self-approach escape occurred**, matching the player's observation. With only 2.2 parked
  minutes the session can neither confirm nor refute the paired check's rate of about
  0.033/min; it shows that the D increase is not visually salient in this session. Two runs of
  repeated escapes in episode 2 (12 in ticks 138-778, 11 in ticks 6052-6474) came from the
  player chasing the fly with the hovering paddle; ESCAPE passes to the ecology unchanged under
  B3a.
- **TRANSIT runs at its configured legacy 9-13 BL/s band**, about 1.4 times EXPLORE, which
  matches "fast but not very fast". This is the magnitude the B2b scale decision keeps
  (`room_kinematic_scale` = 1.0, ROOM about 5-15 times slower than the reviewed free-flight
  measurements in body-normalized terms, `game/M1_8_B_PLAN.md` section 2), not a B3a effect.
  A TRANSIT sampling stage that holds the envelope, as B2b-ii and B2b-iii did, would not make
  it faster; nothing is tuned because of this observation.
- Hit rate and survival are not computed (check 8).

## 10. Status

All preregistered checks pass, including the local Windows checks and the recommended human
playtest. **The user accepted B3a on 2026-10-21** and decided to leave TRANSIT speed unchanged
for now. The D increase in section 6 is accepted as a tracked consequence: the fly spends
about a quarter of its airborne time in TRANSIT at 9-13 BL/s, still driven by the legacy
shared 8 s sinusoid, and approaches a parked paddle more often. The accepted runtime branch is
`feature/m1-8-b3a`; `feature/m1-8-b2b-iii` @ `c4f9b8f` stays as the previous accepted runtime.

Not done, deliberately: TRANSIT or other new speed sampling, timer removal or new bout
models, and any change to the policy, the decoder, the lifecycle or its threat input, the
recorder schema, the caps or the scale.

**Incident during the local checks (not caused by B3a).** Removing the B2b-iii acceptance
worktree with `git worktree remove --force` followed its directory junctions and emptied the
main checkout's `data\` and `artifacts\results\` (the cleanup command came from the
assistant; the safe procedure is now in `results/game/M1_8_B2B_III.md` section 11). FlyBrain
re-downloaded `data\`, and both files match the SHA256 values recorded in
`results/manifest.json`. The original experiment's 55 arrays were regenerated into
`artifacts\results\` with `experiment.py` (four Numba threads, outputs to
`artifacts\regen\results`, so the protected `results\` was not touched); every per-seed
accuracy equals the protected record, and `verify_results.py` passes. The private M2.4-B
data in the `m2-learning` worktree (146 files) were not affected.
