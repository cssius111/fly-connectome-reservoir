## What remains unconstrained or arbitrary

No matched evidence fixes the eight current state-speed envelopes, airborne/perched time ratio, feeding residence, voluntary takeoff hazard, recovery duration, odor-concentration thresholds, history horizon, bilateral steering gain or landing commitment probability.

The strongest examples of invented current timing are the shared 8 s speed sinusoid, uniform explore/transit timers, forced 8 s odor-bout expiry, 4 s odor ignoring, 6 s search expiry, 3.6 s casting sine, 0.9 s approach timeout, 8 s landing cooldown and 7/13 s curvature periods. The audit classifies each config parameter as direct numerical evidence (A), quantitatively phenomenological (B), engineering/game (C), or placeholder (D). **No current ecological numerical parameter is promoted to A.** These R0 letters are distinct from the architectural categories in existing code comments.

Important code findings:

- `LAND_OR_PERCH` is a moving approach placeholder. There is no supported touchdown/perch, feeding or takeoff implementation.
- Local odor derivative and bilateral difference are generated but unused by ecology.
- Current search oscillates around upwind, not necessarily across the wind. Current wind is an ideal local ambient-flow estimate and exerts no physical force.
- The landing expansion value differentiates a weighted affordance score; it is not angular retinal expansion.
- During active neural actions/pulses, WORLD can replace ecological speed targets with the baseline target. Logged state envelopes must not be mistaken for applied or actual speed.
- With curvature enabled, configured random wander is superseded as the drift source.

These are documented model limitations, not new defects authorizing changes to frozen M1.7.1.

## Proposed M1.8 architecture

Use a hierarchy of phenomenological context/history, legal local sensory evidence, a locomotor mode manager with navigation/landing modules, and fly-side actuation. Apply the existing neural threat override before kinematics.

The minimum observable repertoire is FLYING, LAND_APPROACH, TOUCHDOWN, PERCHED, FEEDING and TAKEOFF. TOUCHDOWN is a contact event/transient; FEEDING can be a supported substate but must remain independently observable. TAKEOFF has voluntary/threat cause tags and distinct profiles. EXPLORE/TRANSIT are flight intent labels; ODOR_TRACK/SURGE/SEARCH are navigation modules. ALERT/ESCAPE remain an orthogonal neural channel. Motivation, confidence, readiness and recovery are preferably bounded continuous variables rather than a proliferation of timer states.

Transitions should depend on local vision/expansion/support, recent odor encounters and losses, wind reliability, feeding contact and legitimate neural threat. Minimum dwell/refractory timers may prevent chatter; a timer alone must not be presented as biological decision making. No food coordinates, mouse position or click state enter the controller. Full circadian sleep and RL remain out of scope.

Current neural attribution stays limited to the modeled Retina -> LC4/LPLC2 -> frozen MaleCNS-derived graph -> descending-neuron readout -> fixed threat/action path. Olfactory, central-complex, initiation and landing circuits are research candidates only. Ecological behavior is not demonstrated to emerge from the connectome.

## Questions to settle before a later implementation

1. What quiet, flight-capable assay and animal cohort should define ROOM's target activity budget and speed distributions?
2. What body-length convention and physical/temporal scaling should be used, including treatment of millisecond launch dynamics?
3. What local evidence separates committed landing, fly-by and abort, and how can supported contact coexist with frozen collision geometry?
4. What distinguishes food contact, feeding and non-feeding perch residence, and what measured distributions constrain voluntary departure?
5. How should ideal wind sensing become uncertainty-aware, and what strategy applies without reliable wind?
6. What free-flight evidence constrains encounter history, bilateral odor use and the joint distribution of saccade angle, duration and interval?
7. Which source statistic definitions and MaleCNS cell/pathway mappings need verification before assigning numerical or neural claims?

The evidence document expands these into twelve exact questions. Where evidence remains insufficient, a later authorized prototype may choose finite, labeled phenomenological bounds with a sensitivity plan. R0 does not select those defaults.
